What’s next.

Events, calls for volunteers and club news. Nothing here requires membership to attend.

Monthly club meeting — visitors welcome

We meet on the 4th Monday of every month at 19:30. Our meeting place changes from month to month, so we confirm the venue by email a few days beforehand. If you are curious about the club, come to one — there is no obligation and nothing to prepare.

Lions Recycle For Sight — give your old glasses a second life

Through the Lions Recycle For Sight programme, used prescription glasses and sunglasses are collected and passed on to people who cannot afford them. Have a pair you no longer wear? Contact us and we will tell you how to hand them in.

New members welcome — there is no age limit

Anyone who wants to serve Limassol is welcome to join, whatever their age. For young people, Lions International also runs Leo clubs for ages 12 to 30. If you want to lead community work rather than just attend it, get in touch.
