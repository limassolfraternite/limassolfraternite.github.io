Track record

What we have actually done.

Real community service across Limassol and its villages in Cyprus — eye and diabetes screenings, blood donation and tree planting. No rounding up, no vanity metrics.

Blood donation drive

Our club took part in the blood donation drive organised by the Lions Region of Limassol — much-needed service, and a fitting way to honour the Lions who came before us.

Health screening in Foini

A community health screening day in the mountain village of Foini, with a strong turnout of residents coming forward for checks.

MedicAlert & Diabetic Village

Over two days, the club supported both the MedicAlert Foundation Cyprus and the Diabetic Village events — practical backing for two causes that keep vulnerable people safer.

Eye & diabetes screenings, Limnati

With the Phoenicians Lions Club and the Cyprus Association for people with Diabetes, we ran eye and diabetes screenings in Limnati, using the Lions Ophthalmology Unit all day. The event brought the club two new members.

Tree planting in Mallia

In Mallia we didn’t just plant trees — we planted a promise of cleaner air and stronger roots for the next generation. Done alongside fellow Lions, with more planting to come.
